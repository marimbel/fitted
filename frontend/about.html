About Fitted

Fashion should feel personal.

Online shopping makes discovering clothing easy — but understanding how that clothing will actually look on you remains difficult. Product photos are shot on models who don't look like you, in lighting that flatters the product, not the buyer.

Fitted is being built to close that gap: a place where discovering a piece and picturing yourself in it happen in the same experience.

Where We're Headed

Discovery, try-on, and personalization — in one place.

Our future goal is to combine three things that today live in separate apps and browser tabs: product discovery, virtual try-on, and a personalized sense of your own style.

Personal

Every recommendation and preview is built around how clothing looks on you — not a generic model.

Effortless

Browsing, selecting, and previewing a look should take seconds, not several open tabs.

Intelligent

Fitted learns your preferences over time to make styling feel more personal.
